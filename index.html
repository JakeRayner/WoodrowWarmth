Underfloor heating

We design, supply and install a range of underfloor heating systems to suit your project. Underfloor heating spreads warmth evenly through your home with consistent temperature regulation, works with all flooring types, and is virtually maintenance-free — as well as up to 25% more energy efficient than traditional radiator systems.

  • Evenly spread, consistent warmth
  • Works with all flooring types
  • Virtually maintenance-free

Flowing screed

Screeding applies the floor base your project needs. We install flowing screed — a liquid product poured to create a smooth, level surface before floor coverings go down — at whatever depth the job demands.

  • Laid thinner than dry screed, leaving room for thicker insulation
  • Greater thermal conductivity — brilliant alongside underfloor heating
  • Takes foot traffic in 24–48 hours so other trades keep moving

Servicing & repairs

Underfloor heating is largely maintenance-free — but if your system ever needs a service or repair, we’re here to help. With over 15 years in the underfloor heating world, we diagnose and fix systems across every kind of property.

  • Fault-finding and diagnosis
  • Commissioning of new systems
  • Ongoing servicing for peace of mind
